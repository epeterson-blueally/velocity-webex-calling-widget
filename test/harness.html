Blind: enter a destination, click Blind (from connected or held) — the call transfers and drops. Consult: click Consult to hold the primary and dial the target; once talking, Complete joins the two calls, or Cancel drops the consult and resumes the primary. If the target declines the primary comes back on hold; if the original caller hangs up mid-consult the consult call is promoted to your active call.
